## Read diagrams in two passes

First, follow the main direction of the arrows. Second, return to the branches, boundaries, and labels. Captions tell you what the diagram is intended to teach.

## Understand mapping labels

- **Direct:** The products have closely comparable primary purposes and behavior.
- **Approximate:** The products solve similar problems, but important differences exist.
- **No direct equivalent:** Another product combination or architecture is needed.

## Use progress without pressure

Lesson completion and bookmarks are stored in your browser. There is no streak and no penalty for returning to an earlier topic. Revisiting material is part of learning.

## Check the evidence

Technical lessons include links to official provider documentation, original project documentation, or standards bodies. A verification date tells you when the comparison was last reviewed.
